24-Hour Life Matrix

24 chronological slots (00:00 to 23:00) with instantaneous toggle between Grid Squircle View and Chronological List View.

Proportional Dynamic Glow Fill

Exact seconds, minutes, and hours calculate precision highlight widths: 3600s fills 100%, 1800s fills 50% with glowing divider accent, and multiple activities render proportional segmented slices.

Multi-Hour Activity Spanning

Activities spanning multiple consecutive hours automatically project across target hour blocks with an Apple pill badge while retaining exact mathematical total hours without duplication.

Live Focus Stopwatch

Top-bar live ticking focus timer. Backed by browser localStorage, persists across reloads, and commits directly into the active hour slot on click.

Global Multilingual AI Engine

The application supports 42 major world languages (Tamil, English, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Chinese, Arabic, Russian, Portuguese, Italian, Korean, and 30+ more). When you change your language in Settings, Habit Intelligence immediately adapts and conducts all conversations and task orchestrations in that language!
